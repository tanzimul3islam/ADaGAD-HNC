2.2. Graphself-supervisedlearning
Self-supervisedlearning(SSL)generatessupervisorysignalsbyminingtheinherentstructureandcorrelationspresentinthedata
[33].Thisparadigmnotonlyeliminatestherelianceonmanualannotationsrequiredbysupervisedlearningbutalsoaddressesthe
lackofsemanticrichnessintraditionalunsupervisedfeaturerepresentations.Ingraphanomalydetection,SSLexploitsintrinsicgraph
cuestobuildsupervisorysignals,enablinglabel-freelearningofnormalpatternsandflaggingdeviationsasanomalies.IngraphSSL,
methodsaretypicallygroupedintothreecategories:generative,contrastive,andhybrid.
Generation-based methods. In graph SSL, generation-basedmethods use features or structuralinformation within the graph
as self-supervisedsignals.Such approachesrely on data reconstructionin GAEs, throughwhich the model acquiressemanticand
structuralrepresentations.VGAE[34]adoptsthevariationalautoencoder(VAE)framework,whichderivesnode-levellatentvectors
throughaGCNencoderandrebuildsthegraphwithaninner-productdecodingscheme.Incontrast,GAAN[35]employsaGenerative
Adversarial Network (GAN) instead of the VAE in VGAE, jointly training the generator, encoder, and discriminator to compute
anomalyscores.Subsequently,GraphMAE[36]masksnodefeaturesandoptimizesthereconstructionprocessusingScaledCosine
Error(SCE),therebyenhancingtherobustnessofgraphrepresentationlearning.Althoughlatentgraphfeaturescanbelearnedthrough
reconstructiontasks,theweakcorrelationbetweenreconstructionobjectivesanddownstreamtasks,togetherwithlimitedaccuracy
incomplexstructures,stillconstrainsperformance.
Contrast-based methods. Contrastive learning-based methods leverage similarities and differences between samples as self-
supervisedsignals.Atitscore,suchmethodsminimizetheembeddingdistanceofsemanticallyrelatedsamplesandmaximizethatof
unrelatedones,whichenablesthemodeltobetterrepresentthegraph’sstructuralandsemanticattributes[37].Forinstance,CoLA
[21] models nodes in attributed networks by contrasting them with their local subgraphs using graph neural networks, enabling
anomalydetectionthroughcontrastiveSSL.Inspiredbythisparadigm,AD-GCL[38]introducesneighborpruningandcompletion,
togetherwithconsistencyconstraintsappliedatbothintra-andinter-viewlevels,whichimproveitsabilitytodetectanomaliesin
both head and tail nodes. Similarly, SCALA [39] incorporates multi-view structure-aware contrastive learning, enabling effective
detection of diverse types of anomalous nodes. In addition, GADMCLG [40] employs neighbor subgraph sampling and subgraph
centralizationstrategiestocaptureanomalouspatternsatbothlocalandgloballevels.Overall,contrastivemethodseffectivelymodel
graphstructuresand,tosomeextent,incorporateattributeinformationforanomalydetection,buttheystillfacelimitationssuchas
limitedcoverageofdiverseanomalytypesandinsufficientrobustnessagainstnoiseandstructuralimbalance.
Hybrid methods. Hybrid graph SSL methods integrate generative and contrastive strategies, combining graph reconstruction
withnodediscriminationtoexploittheircomplementarystrengths.Forexample,Sub-CR[41]usesmulti-viewcontrastivelearning
togetherwithreconstructionofattributesfromneighboringnodestomoreeffectivelyidentifynode-levelanomalies.Subsequently,
DualGAD [42] adjusts subgraph construction and contrastive mechanisms within a similar framework and reconstructs subgraph
attributes and structures to broaden anomaly detection coverage. DSLAD [43], in contrast to the coupled designs of Sub-CR and
DualGAD,disentanglesrepresentationlearningfromanomalydiscriminationandestablishesabalancedsemanticspacetoreduce
semantic overlap and class imbalance. Despite these advances, existing approaches fail to capture view differences in multi-view
contrastwithsufficientgranularity.Furthermore,theencoderintheattributereconstructionmodulehaslimitedexpressivepower,
makingitdifficulttofullycapturecomplexattributecorrelationsandfine-grainedfeaturedifferences.

contrastivelearningstrategy,includingnode-to-nodeandnode-to-subgraphcontrastiveobjectives,ourmethodeffectivelycaptures
complex structural and attribute anomalies. Results on ten widely used benchmarks show that GADREMC outperforms state-of-
the-art baselines in detection accuracy, demonstrating the effectiveness of the combined reconstruction-and-contrastive strategy.
Subsequent research will aim to devise graph augmentation approaches that are both effective and scalable, strengthening the
model’sgeneralizationabilityinrealisticenvironments.Additionally,weplantoextendthishybridframeworktodynamicattributed
